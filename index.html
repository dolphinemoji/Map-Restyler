Map Restyler

OpenStreetMap SVG in, Blank-style map out. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

Drop OpenStreetMap SVG exports here or click to choose files. You can also drop name.labels.json, name.highlight.json or a map_style.json settings file.
Style settings

Change a color and every map on the page updates. Settings are remembered in this browser. Use Save settings to keep a copy or share it; it's the same map_style.json the Python version uses.
